PROJECT 01 PCB DESIGN · AUTOMOTIVE TESTING

ECU power management
& testing system

A system for International Motors Mexico that powers four ECUs, monitors their CAN messages and electrical behaviour, and runs automated tests.

KiCadESP32 / MicroPythonCANVISAOscilloscope
Designed & built
I owned the circuit design, PCB layout, component calculations, assembly and soldering: one replaceable board per ECU with independent battery, accessory and ignition lines, using MOSFET switching for fast, repeated operation without contact wear.
Validated
I worked through failure scenarios and tested extended operation, repeated switching, CAN reception from real ECUs and sensor readings against lab instruments, measuring switching response times with an oscilloscope.

RESULT Selected for ExpoIngenierías and demonstrated live with International ECUs. Hardware and documentation were handed over to International.

Read the full story +

7th semester · Weekly reviews with two International engineers guided the development. The system combined ESP32 firmware in MicroPython with a Python computer interface developed mainly by my teammates.

Working without university funding shaped the hardware: through-hole components and separate CAN and sensing modules. I also proposed a smaller integrated PCB combining the ESP32, CAN circuitry and measurement chips; it remained a design for future development. Safety features included text-based test sequences, a main fuse, shutdown on over-voltage and a fail-safe if the computer disconnected.

Due to International’s confidentiality policy, ECU-specific CAN messages, VISA command sequences and detailed test results cannot be shown. View ExpoIngenierías certificate ↗

PROJECT 02 INDUSTRIAL ROBOTICS · PLC · HMI

Robotic assembly station
— MIT FrED Factory

Rebuilding Station 3 of the MIT FrED Factory line, where a KUKA robot handles and presses together the funnel assembly of a filament extrusion device.

Siemens PLCTIA PortalKUKAHMI
Rebuilt the controls
With previous PLC programs unavailable, I corrected the KUKA interface wiring and signals, replaced the Siemens + Allen-Bradley arrangement with one Siemens PLC in ladder logic, and rewrote the robot sequence from scratch around the new fixtures.
Made it operable
I developed an HMI with process progress, timers, robot status, sensor states and missing-part warnings, plus start/stop/continue controls, partial resets, door interlocks and robot-status checks.
Read the full story +

6th semester · Without the usual robot programming software, I wrote the operating logic directly as code from recorded positions and transferred it to the controller manually. Updated paths, speeds and intermediate positions improved pickup, placement and pressing. The station added alignment guides, a redesigned 3D-printed gripper and gravity-fed part storage with stock and base detection sensors.

Fun fact: in the same course, a small project with Schneider Electric Mexico simulating a circuit-breaker stamping process won a participant-voted award. See the LinkedIn post ↗

PROJECT 03 RESEARCH COLLABORATION

Embedded vision &
robot control platform

A tracked robot built for a master’s research project on TinyML-based path following. I developed the hardware and software infrastructure for testing future models.

STM32 NUCLEO-F446RERaspberry Pi 5OpenCVDocker
Sensing & control
I adapted a Madgwick sensor-fusion implementation for the MPU9250 with DMA-based IMU acquisition, calibration and filtering to reduce drift, together with encoder acquisition, PI motor control and fail-safe behaviour.

RESULT At handover, sensing, video streaming, browser controls and movement were all working, ready for the model-integration stage.

PROJECT 04 EMBEDDED · MOTOR CONTROL · SENSOR FUSION

Autonomous
tractor-inspired robot

Control software and electronics for a robot that drives a square route over rough, gapped tiles and returns to its start point and heading.

STM32 / CMATLABSystem identificationPI / PID
Modelled & tuned
I used PRBS test inputs to record the motors’ response, built and cross-validated a plant model, and tuned the PI speed controllers with MATLAB’s PID tools.
Sensor fusion
I wrote an MPU9250 calibration routine (static and figure-eight), applied low-pass filtering and adjusted sensor weights between straights and turns. The robot could straighten its heading after being pushed.
Read the full story +

5th semester, Industrial Automation course. I also made molds and cast silicone wheels to improve grip and reduce bouncing. The robot used a laser-cut acrylic base, a 3D-printed tractor-style body, L298N drivers and 18650 batteries with separate supplies for logic and motors. I prioritised reliable movement and heading correction over speed.

PROJECT 05 MECHANICAL DESIGN · MOTION CONTROL

Mini mechatronic
foosball table

A mini foosball robot that took 2nd place out of 15+ designs in the class tournament.

ArduinoGear designPI control3D printingPower electronics
Designed & built
My custom gear system moved the player faster than direct-drive stepper designs. A hardware-timer PWM drove the stepper through a TMC2209 driver, so the robot could move and strike with a 12 V solenoid at the same time.
Complete system
I designed the CAD parts (printed in PLA) and the MDF case, and built a joystick controller, distance-sensor goal counting, a 16×2 LCD scoreboard and limit switches. For extra credit I built an AC–DC supply with 12 V and 5 V outputs.
Read the full story +

4th semester, Implementation of Mechatronic Systems. A PI controller made the movement smoother. I tested and refined movement, shooting and goal detection by adjusting both hardware and code. The power supply had enough extra capacity that I reused it in later projects. Inspired by the Automated Foosball Table concept, with my own design and component choices.

PROJECT 06 MEASUREMENT · SIGNAL ANALYSIS

AC frequency
measurement system

A power-supply monitor that tracks frequency changes around 60 Hz as loads are added or removed, housed in a lightweight load-bearing case.

ArduinoPython / NumPy / FFTMatplotlibSolidWorks
Measured & analysed
An Arduino with an ACS712 sensor collected 800-reading batches and sent them to Python over serial. I used NumPy’s FFT for frequency estimation, with a live Matplotlib view, a small display and LED threshold alerts.
Designed & tested
The case had to support 100 kg within an MDF weight limit. I designed it in SolidWorks, checked weak points with structural analysis, then cut and assembled it. In an informal test it held two adults (~220 kg) without failure.
Read the full story +

Mechatronic Integration course. The project combined electronics, software and mechanical design in a single instrument for observing how the supply responds to changing electrical loads.
